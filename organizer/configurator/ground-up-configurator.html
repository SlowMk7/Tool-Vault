How to measure

  • Measure inside, wall to wall, in the middle of each side.
  • Measure width at the bottom and again at the top. If they differ, tick the slope box.
  • Check nothing sticks into the drawer: rails, lips or the cabinet frame above.
  • Not sure? Choose "Need help designing?" at the Order step.

Design your compartments

Drag the yellow handles to move a wall. Tap ⊕ beside a row to try one more compartment in it. Tap a compartment, then the red × to remove it, or use the panel below to resize or rename it. The front of the drawer is at the bottom.
